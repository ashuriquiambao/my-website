Welcome to NIKEDO

Smart Ideas for Everyday Technology

NIKEDO is a student innovation project created to explore how technology can make everyday tasks easier, smarter, and more convenient.

Our project focuses on developing a future laptop concept that can automatically close when the user is no longer detected in front of it.

Our Main Idea

A laptop equipped with a camera or sensor can detect whether the user is still present. If the user leaves for one minute, the laptop can automatically close itself.
