About NIKEDO

NIKEDO is a student innovation project focused on creating smart, practical, and convenient technology for everyday life.

💡 What is NIKEDO?

NIKEDO is an innovative student project that explores how technology can be used to create useful solutions for everyday problems. Our main concept is a laptop that can automatically close when the user is no longer present in front of the device.

🎯 Our Purpose

The purpose of NIKEDO is to develop a creative technology concept that combines convenience, automation, and user awareness. Through this project, we aim to show how simple technology can be developed into a useful and practical innovation.

🚀 Our Vision

Our vision is to create innovative ideas that can make technology more convenient and helpful in everyday life. NIKEDO represents our goal of combining creativity, technology, and practical solutions.

✨ Why NIKEDO?

NIKEDO was created to demonstrate how students can use technology and creativity to develop new ideas. The project encourages innovation and problem-solving while exploring how automation can improve the way people interact with technology.
