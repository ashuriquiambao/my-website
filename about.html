About NIKEDO

Learn more about our group and our purpose.

Who We Are

NIKEDO is a student innovation group created to develop a practical and creative technology idea. Our goal is to explore how technology can help make everyday activities easier, safer, and more convenient.

Our Purpose

Our project aims to combine creativity and technology to create an innovative laptop concept. We want to show how simple automated features can improve the way people use technology in their daily lives.

Why We Created NIKEDO

We created NIKEDO as part of our student innovation project. Through this project, we can apply what we have learned in ICT while developing an idea that can potentially be improved and developed in the future.
